Common Questions

What footage format should I send?
+

We accept all major formats — MP4, MOV, MXF, R3D. Upload via the secure link we provide after checkout. Higher resolution is always better.

How do revisions work?
+

Each package includes the number of revisions listed. Submit all your notes in a single round for the fastest turnaround.

Can I request a rush delivery?
+

Yes — rush delivery is available for an additional fee depending on the package. Reach out before ordering to confirm availability.

Do you handle music licensing?
+

All edits use fully licensed music cleared for commercial use on major platforms including YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok.

What if I need more than one video?
+

You can order multiple packages at once, or consider our Black Label subscription if you're producing consistently high volume.
