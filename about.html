About Us

Built To Elevate
Real Estate Media.

Cliffside Cuts is a premium video editing studio built specifically for real estate media companies and agents who refuse to settle for ordinary.

Our Story

Where It Started.

Cliffside Cuts was founded after watching too many real estate videos miss the mark — technically competent but creatively flat. We built a studio around one idea: that property media should do more than document a space. It should sell a feeling.

From a single editor and a handful of clients, we've grown into a full editorial operation serving some of the most respected real estate media companies in the country. Every system we've built, every editor we've trained, points back to that original belief.

The name comes from the idea of standing at the edge — of what's possible, of what great editing can do. We live there.
